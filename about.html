About Proxo

Make your next customer a little closer.

Proxo is an advertising platform designed specifically for TikTok advertising in the Kurdistan Region and Iraq.

01 / REACH

Promote with confidence

Businesses, sellers, and product owners can promote their products and services on TikTok and reach hundreds of potential customers in a simple and convenient way.

02 / CONNECT

One place to connect

Users can create their own dedicated contact page, bringing their contact information and communication methods together so customers can reach them quickly and easily.

03 / MEASURE

Understand your growth

Proxo lets users view their advertising statistics in real time, making it easier to follow the performance and reach of their advertisements.

Advertising should be accessible, clear, and convenient.

Our goal is to make TikTok advertising more accessible, clear, and convenient for businesses across the Kurdistan Region and Iraq, while helping them introduce their products and services to a wider audience.

Proxo — A simpler way to connect your business with more customers through TikTok.
